N. MAKE CONSENSUS AND CROSS-EXAMINATION DETERMINISTIC
======================================================================

Replace the effective architecture:

N proposers → one verifier → one judge

with:

N proposers
→ multiple cross-examiners
→ multiple verifiers/falsifiers
→ deterministic evidence aggregation
→ constrained judge

For every high or critical candidate, require:

- at least 2 independent verifier/falsifier lineages;
- preferably 3 for critical candidates;
- explicit SUPPORT, DISPUTE or INCONCLUSIVE decisions;
- exact source evidence;
- checked assumptions;
- checked reachability;
- checked controls;
- lineage identity.

A single conservative verifier must not silently eliminate a candidate.

One verifier may:

- add a dispute;
- lower confidence;
- force manual review;
- request more evidence.

One verifier alone may not deterministically reject a candidate unless it
provides decisive source-bound contradictory evidence.

Use a deterministic decision matrix:

1. CONFIRMED

   Requires qualifying deterministic reproduction, formal counterexample or
   equivalent proof, plus integrity validation.

2. STRONGLY_SUPPORTED

   Requires multiple independent supporting lineages, validated reachability
   and no unresolved decisive contradiction.

3. NEEDS_MANUAL_REVIEW

   Used when credible independent evidence is split or incomplete.

4. REJECTED

   Requires decisive deterministic contradiction or at least two independent
   evidence-backed falsifiers with no stronger supporting evidence.

If two independent lineages dispute a high/critical candidate with validated
evidence:

- the preliminary status must reflect the dispute;
- it cannot remain silently unaffected until the judge;
- downgrade to NEEDS_MANUAL_REVIEW or REJECTED according to the evidence
  matrix.

If cross-examiners support a candidate:

- that may increase review priority and evidence support;
- it still cannot bypass the confirmation cap.

The judge may only preserve or lower the deterministic cap.
